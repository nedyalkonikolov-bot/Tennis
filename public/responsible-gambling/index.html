Responsible gambling

Use tennis predictions as research, never as certainty

TennisTipz is for adults only. Betting involves risk, and every tennis prediction can lose. Never bet more than you can afford to lose, never chase losses, and do not use betting to solve financial problems.

Safer betting rules

  • Only bet where it is legal for you to do so.
  • Set a fixed budget before opening a sportsbook.
  • Skip matches when odds, player news, or data quality are unclear.
  • Take breaks and stop immediately if betting feels stressful or compulsive.
  • Do not treat TennisTipz, AI models, or market odds as guaranteed advice.

Warning signs

If you are hiding betting, borrowing money to bet, chasing losses, or feeling unable to stop, seek help from a qualified gambling support service in your country.

Why player stats matter

Tennis betting markets react to name value, recent results, injuries, surface changes, travel, and tournament pressure. A useful prediction page should not rely on odds alone. Player stats help explain why a price may be strong, weak, or already over-adjusted.

TennisTipz is built around ATP and WTA separation because men's and women's tours have different ranking structures, match rhythms, and market behavior. Keeping the player stats split makes comparison cleaner and more useful.

Stats to check before a tennis bet

Ranking and points show level. Recent match results show momentum. Headline news can explain sudden odds changes. Live status can reveal whether a price belongs to a current match or an upcoming market. Together, these signals create a better foundation for tennis betting predictions.
